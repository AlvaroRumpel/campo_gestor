<objective>
Build the DG mass-entry section on the ATF detail screen — the phone-in-the-corral surface that closes
REPR-03 and makes REPR-04's percentage move.

Purpose: the field reality driving every decision here is that a vet passes dozens of vacas through
the chute in one session. Any flow that opens a dialog per animal is unusable. That is why there is
one session date, one list, three chips per row, and one save.
Output: the `_DgSection` block of `AtfDetailScreen` plus its tests.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Staged local state → `save_dg_records` payload | The client controls every animal id, result, date, and observation in the batch |
| Open screen → concurrent edits elsewhere | The row list can be stale: another vet may have removed an animal since the screen loaded |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-42 | Tampering | A stale client submitting a DG for an animal removed from the ATF in another session | medium | mitigate | `save_dg_records`'s membership existence guard (05-03) rejects the record and rolls the whole batch back, surfacing the DG-save failure copy |
| T-05-43 | Tampering | A partially applied batch leaving half the herd recorded and half not | medium | mitigate | The RPC body is one transaction (05-03); the client never issues per-row calls |
| T-05-44 | Elevation of Privilege | A reader or owner saving DGs by calling the RPC directly, past the omitted save button | high | mitigate | `save_dg_records` re-checks `get_role(...) = 'veterinarian'` and raises 42501 (05-03) |
| T-05-45 | Tampering | A forged `result` value outside the three allowed | medium | mitigate | The RPC raises 22023 and `dg_records_result_check` (05-01) is a database CHECK; the client only ever emits `DgResult.dbValue` |
| T-05-46 | Repudiation | A correction silently erasing what the vet originally recorded | high | mitigate | The RPC only INSERTs and 05-01 grants no UPDATE or DELETE policy on `dg_records`; the UI stages a new record rather than editing the old one |
| T-05-47 | Tampering | An `exam_date` shifted by a day through timezone conversion | medium | mitigate | Dates are serialised date-only (`toIso8601String().substring(0, 10)`) into a Postgres `date` column, per the repository contract in 05-02 |
| T-05-48 | Denial of Service | A very large herd producing an oversized single payload | low | accept | Only changed rows are sent, and herd size is bounded in practice; the E6 backstop test exercises 200 animals |
</threat_model>
